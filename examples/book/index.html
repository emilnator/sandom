Editing prints the document across the book's numbered pages.

Drag a page or a cover to turn it; let go and it falls under its own weight. Drag the table to orbit, hold Space and drag to pan, scroll to zoom. Arrow keys orbit; a tap of Space or Page Down and Page Up turn pages. With the editor's document printed, click, select and type in a page's text block; drag its margins to turn it.
